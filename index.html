The important differences at a glance

Use this as a map before reading the instruction tables.

QuestionSGXTDXSEV-SNP
Protected unitEnclave within a processTrust Domain: a confidential VMSNP confidential VM
Primary CPU structuresEPC pages, EPCM metadata, SECS and TCSSEAM/TDX module, Secure EPT, PAMT and private KeyIDsAMD-SP, VMSA, NPT and RMP
Main call boundaryENCLS by privileged software; ENCLU by user/enclave codeSEAMCALL by host; TDCALL by guest TDAMD-SP firmware commands and guest GHCB / VMGEXIT
